150-point sweep and Public Transit's 50-point first look) - does the same
"threshold dominates, all three metrics move together" pattern hold on a
third, differently-mixed scene, at closer to full sample size this time.

this is a genuinely different starting point again, not another harder

Three scenes in, with meaningfully different baselines and mix content,
the pattern hasn't broken once: threshold dominates (r consistently
-0.93 to -0.95), and the three outcome metrics move together almost
perfectly (r consistently >=0.98) regardless of scene. This looks like a
property of the ducking architecture itself, not an artifact of any one
scene's specific audio content.
