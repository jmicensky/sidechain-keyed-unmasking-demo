LATIN HYPERCUBE PARAMETER SWEEP -- FINDINGS
Construction Scene, Dialogue priority class, 150 points x {Basic, Advanced}
Search ranges: threshold -60 to -18dB, ratio 1.5-10:1, knee 0-24dB,
attack 1-50ms, release 20-400ms (Latin Hypercube design, seed=42)
Data: analysis/lhs_sweep_results.csv
Script: analysis/run_lhs_sweep.py
======================================================================

GOAL: find settings maximizing mean margin gain, STOI gain, and
psychoacoustic partial-loudness reduction, and specifically look for a
"sweet spot" where STOI/loudness gains are high while margin gain moves
comparatively little (more intelligibility improvement than the raw
level-based margin metric alone would suggest).

FINDING 1 -- No such sweet spot exists among these three metrics; they
are almost perfectly correlated with each other.
  Across all 150 Advanced-mode points: STOI gain vs. margin gain r=0.994;
  partial-loudness reduction vs. margin gain r=0.994. Fitting a straight
  line through each relationship and looking at the residuals (how far a
  point sits from that typical trend) finds only tiny deviations - at
  most about +0.015 STOI-gain and a few percentage points of loudness
  reduction above what margin gain alone would predict, against ranges of
  0.002-0.114 and 0-11.4pp respectively. In practical terms: settings that
  improve mean margin gain improve STOI and partial-loudness reduction
  right along with it, in essentially fixed proportion. There is no
  parameter combination in this search that gets you meaningfully more
  perceived intelligibility improvement without a proportional increase
  in the raw margin change too - all three metrics are measuring
  consequences of the same underlying "how much was the masker actually
  ducked within Dialogue's band," just through different lenses, which is
  itself a reassuring cross-validation of the three metrics rather than a
  disappointing result.

FINDING 2 -- Threshold dominates; the other four parameters barely
matter on their own.
  Correlation with Advanced-mode margin gain, each parameter alone:
    threshold   r = -0.929  (by far the strongest predictor)
    release     r = +0.186
    attack      r = -0.170
    ratio       r = +0.132
    knee        r = +0.066
  Binned by threshold (Advanced mode):
    thr [-60,-54): margin gain mean +6.77dB (max +6.96dB)  n=21
    thr [-54,-48): margin gain mean +6.50dB (max +6.94dB)  n=22
    thr [-48,-42): margin gain mean +5.73dB (max +6.88dB)  n=21
    thr [-42,-36): margin gain mean +4.06dB (max +6.21dB)  n=22
    thr [-36,-30): margin gain mean +2.30dB (max +5.96dB)  n=21
    thr [-30,-24): margin gain mean +0.52dB (max +2.40dB)  n=22
    thr [-24,-18): margin gain mean +0.11dB (max +1.02dB)  n=21
  Ratio/knee/attack/release still matter - the spread within each
  threshold bin (e.g. +5.94 to +6.96dB in the lowest bin) shows they add
  real variation - just much less than threshold does.

FINDING 3 -- The performance curve is STILL RISING at the edge of the
tested range (-60dB); this search did not find where it saturates.
  This search only covered -60 to -18dB. Extending below -60dB would
  very likely continue to improve all three metrics further, based on the
  trend - this was not tested and should be if a truly maximal setting is
  wanted.

FINDING 4 -- The real tradeoff worth navigating is threshold vs. how much
of the clip the sidechain actually engages on, which directly conflicts
with the earlier "transparent when Dialogue is silent" goal.
  Measured directly (50ms-smoothed envelope of the dry Dialogue stem
  crossing each threshold):
    threshold -60dB: sidechain engaged 70.4% of the clip
    threshold -54dB: sidechain engaged 62.1% of the clip
    threshold -48dB: sidechain engaged 51.0% of the clip
    threshold -42dB: sidechain engaged 34.8% of the clip
    threshold -36dB: sidechain engaged 16.6% of the clip
    threshold -30dB: sidechain engaged  3.4% of the clip
    threshold -24dB: sidechain engaged  0.0% of the clip
  The settings that maximize the three metrics do so specifically by
  having the compressor engaged for the MAJORITY of the clip (51-70%),
  not just during clearly loud/present dialogue - directly at odds with
  wanting the mix untouched when the priority sound isn't making noise.
  This is a genuine tradeoff to make deliberately, not a free optimum.

RECOMMENDED OPERATING POINTS (Advanced/Summed-bus, Construction Scene,
Dialogue priority - real points from the sweep, not interpolated):

  AGGRESSIVE (maximize the three metrics; compressor active most of the time)
    Threshold -58.6dB | Ratio 7.4:1 | Knee 5.9dB | Attack 3.2ms | Release 215ms
    Mean margin gain +6.96dB | STOI gain +0.104 | Loudness reduction +11.4pp
    Sidechain engaged 68.8% of the clip

  BALANCED (majority of the available gain, much less constantly engaged)
    Threshold -40.3dB | Ratio 5.7:1 | Knee 22.9dB | Attack 16.9ms | Release 347ms
    Mean margin gain +6.21dB | STOI gain +0.100 | Loudness reduction +9.8pp
    Sidechain engaged 29.9% of the clip

  CONSERVATIVE (maximize time spent fully transparent; modest but real gain)
    Threshold -28.8dB | Ratio 7.7:1 | Knee 9.4dB | Attack 8.5ms | Release 384ms
    Mean margin gain +2.40dB | STOI gain +0.042 | Loudness reduction +3.0pp
    Sidechain engaged 1.9% of the clip

Given Finding 2, ratio/knee/attack/release at any of these three tiers
could reasonably be swapped for other values within the tested ranges
without changing the outcome much - these are real tested points, not a
claim that these exact decimal values are individually load-bearing.
